# Smart Home Device Hub & Automation Guide

## Connected Devices
- **Living Room Lumina Lights:** Hue Smart Bulbs (Brightness: 80%, Color: Warm White 2700K). Voice trigger: "turn living room lights on/off" or "set living room to cozy".
- **Office Airflow Thermostat:** Ecobee Smart Thermostat. Default comfort temperature is set to 21.5°C (71°F). Eco mode engages automatically after 10 PM.
- **Front Porch Ultra Lock:** Yale Smart Deadbolt with PIN & biometric unlock. Auto-locks after 3 minutes of inactivity. Current battery is at 92%.
- **Kitchen Sonos One Speaker:** Multi-room audio enabled. Max volume safety cap set to 75%.

## Preset Home Scenes
1. **Movie Night Scene:** Dims living room lights to 15%, turns off kitchen lights, closes motorized blackout blinds, and sets TV backlighting to deep blue.
2. **Good Morning Scene:** Gradually ramps bedroom & hallway lighting over 10 minutes, adjusts thermostat to 22°C, and broadcasts the daily calendar briefing.
3. **Away / Armed Scene:** Shuts down all interior lighting, lowers thermostat to 18°C, locks all doors, and arms motion sensors.
